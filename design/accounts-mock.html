Set up your paperbot

About two minutes, with the board plugged into your Mac.

  1. Install the setup tool
    curl -fsSL https://pocketbot.ca/install | sh
  2. Plug in your paperbot and run the setup
    pocketbot setup
    It installs the firmware, gives the board your Mac's Wi-Fi, and shows a code.
  3. Enter the code from your terminal
