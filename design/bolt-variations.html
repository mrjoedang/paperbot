Bolt: variations + hybrids

You picked Bolt, so here are 8 Bolt variants plus 4 hybrids with Pip and Poster. Every screen is a real 200×200 black/white buffer, the same as the panel. Each Bolt variant ends with an expression sheet showing exactly which face parts (bitmaps) it needs. Click any screen to download a 1-bit PNG. Star favourites and add notes; they're saved in this browser.

Size

Animation rule: the screen only animates while the device is awake and busy (the recording seconds counter and sending/thinking). Ready is light sleep: one frozen frame with zero refreshes. Results, queued, too-short and no-SD are single static frames before it sleeps again. A result may flash once on arrival; that one-off flash isn't simulated here.


  
Device fonts: a classic 5×7 bitmap font at integer scales, plus (Poster hybrids and Glyph Bolt) a heavy sans run through a hard 1-bit threshold. Icons are 16×16 1-bit sprites scaled by whole numbers.
