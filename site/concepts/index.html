pocketbot.ca concepts

Warm white, soft charcoal, one typeface, the same 3D model.

  • O4. Geist SerifThe current site (Object) set in Geist Serif + Geist. Its variable axis turns every dot into a different shape, so the dots follow the device: round, square while recording, triangle while sending, hearts when it's filed.
  • O1. Real buttonObject + Soft. The device is the hero, turned so its side button faces you. Press and hold that button on the 3D model itself.
  • O2. Spoken headlineObject + Soft. The headline is what Bolt is hearing, written word by word while the screen records, then filed. Loops, with a pause button.
  • O3. DockObject + Soft. The big device tucks itself into the corner as you scroll, then reacts to "a day with paperbot" as you read.
  • A. ObjectThe device alone in the centre with a "Hold to talk" button under it. Short text, lots of space.
  • A2. CasualRecursive. The headline relaxes into its casual style while you hold the button, a little waveform plays, and what you "said" types itself out.
  • A3. SoftFraunces at full softness. A round button with the device's ☀ glyph and a ring that fills while you hold, a gentle float, and a memo card that settles in.
  • A4. TurnBricolage Grotesque. Scroll and the device makes one full turn while huge quiet words change behind it: Hold, Talk, Let go, Done, Your turn.
  • B. MarginText on the left, the device fixed on the right. As you scroll, it turns and its screen follows the story.
  • C. TourA short list of parts beside the device. Pick one and the device turns to show it.
