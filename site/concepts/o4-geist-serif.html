Press and hold, say something, let go.

For the thoughts you’d otherwise lose.

paperbot is a small e-paper robot for your pocket. Hold its button, say the thing, let go. It becomes a note, a todo, a reminder, or a question it goes and researches.

Hold and talk.

The button on its side wakes it and starts recording in the same moment. No app, no wake word, nothing to unlock.

Let go.

Your words go to your own server and are written down in about two seconds. Then the device forgets its copy.

It’s filed.

Notes, todos and reminders, each with a title and the right time. Questions come back researched, with sources.

Everything lands in one quiet list.

On your phone or laptop. Tick off todos, see what’s due, read the answers, and play back what you said when you need to.

The details.

Size
39.8 × 53 × 16.9 mm
Screen
1.54 inch e-paper, 200 × 200
Battery
About 3½ days at five memos a day, about two weeks if left alone
Offline
