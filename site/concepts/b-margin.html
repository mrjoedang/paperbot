Say it once. Then forget about it.

paperbot is a small e-paper robot for your pocket. Hold its button, say what's on your mind, and let go. It turns what you said into a note, a todo, a reminder, or a question it researches for you.

How it worksNo app, no wake word, nothing to unlock.

Hold the button and talk.

The button on the side wakes it and starts recording in the same moment, with a small beep. Say it however it comes out.

Let go.

The clip goes straight to your own server and is transcribed in about two seconds. Once it's safely there, the device deletes its copy.

It sorts itself.

Each memo is filed as a note, a todo, a reminder or a question, with a title written for you. "In five minutes" becomes an actual time. If it didn't catch what you said, it tells you instead of guessing.

Questions come back answered.

Ask it to look something up and a research agent reads the sources and writes back with links, on a small daily budget. The short answer shows up on the screen.
