Press and hold.

A small place to put a passing thought.

paperbot is a pocket-sized e-paper robot. Hold its button, say the thing, let go. It becomes a note, a todo, a reminder, or a question it goes and researches.

Hold and talk

The side button wakes it and starts recording in the same moment. No app, no wake word.

Let go

The clip goes to your own server and is transcribed in about two seconds.

It's filed

Notes, todos and reminders land in one quiet list. Questions come back researched, with sources.

Details

Size
39.8 × 53 × 16.9 mm
Screen
