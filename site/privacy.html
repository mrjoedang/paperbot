Privacy

Updated 14 November 2026

Paperbot is a small open-source project run by Joe Dang. This page covers the hosted service at app.paperbot.ca: what it keeps, why, and how to delete it.

What it keeps

  • Your account: your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, Paperbot asks Google only for your basic profile and email.
  • Your memos: the audio you record, its transcript, and what’s made from it: the title, the kind (to-do, reminder, note, research), due dates and research results.
  • Your Paperbot: its ID, firmware version, battery level, diagnostic logs, and the Wi-Fi networks you add. Wi-Fi passwords are stored encrypted.
  • What you connect: the assistants you connect (such as Claude or ChatGPT) and your own AI provider key if you add one. The key is stored encrypted.

How it’s used

Only to run Paperbot for you: to transcribe and sort what you say, show it in your list, and research it when you ask. Your data isn’t sold, isn’t used for ads, and isn’t used to train AI models.

Who handles it

  • Cloudflare hosts the service and stores your data. Transcription and sorting run on Cloudflare’s AI models.
  • Research happens only the way you set it up: with your own Anthropic key, the memo is sent to Anthropic; with your own assistant, that assistant reads the memos you ask it to.
  • Google is used only to sign you in, if you choose it.
  • Stripe takes payment if you buy credit. Paperbot never sees your card; it keeps only what you bought and when.

How long, and deleting it

Everything is kept until you delete it. You can delete any memo, download all your data, or delete your account in the app’s settings. Deleting your account removes your recordings, transcripts, results, devices and sign-in.
