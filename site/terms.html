Paperbot is a small open-source project run by Joe Dang. These terms cover the hosted service at app.paperbot.ca.

The service

Paperbot is free to use within a daily allowance. It is provided as is, without a guarantee that it is always available or that every transcript, answer or report is right. Don’t rely on it for anything urgent or safety-critical.

Prepaid credit

  • Credit is sold in packs of CA$10 through Stripe. It pays for AI use past the free allowance: memos, research and app builds. The app shows your balance, and each memo’s cost comes off it.
  • Credit doesn’t expire while your account exists. It has no cash value and can’t be transferred.
  • Unused credit is refunded on request. Email support@paperbot.ca. Credit already used isn’t refunded.
  • Prices and the free allowance may change. Credit you already bought keeps its value.

Your account

Don’t use Paperbot to break the law or to abuse the service, such as by making accounts to get around the limits. Accounts that do may be closed, and their unused credit refunded.
